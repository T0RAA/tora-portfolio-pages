The starting point

Explore music discovery through shared sonic identity and live listening presence.

What I contributed

Built a music social platform that matches listeners through their sonic identity. Architected the ML engine, integrated Spotify, and shipped an iOS client with Capacitor.

How it works

CLAP audio embeddings represent sonic similarities. WebSockets support live listening presence, while a PyTorch retraining pipeline supports the ML engine. FastAPI connects these systems to the application.

What came out of it

  • Built matching through shared sonic identity and live listening presence.
  • Integrated Spotify across its 100M+ track catalog; this describes catalog coverage, not platform users.
  • Shipped an iOS client with Capacitor.

The bigger possibility

Help music become a starting point for connection, giving listeners a way to discover both sounds and people. Community outcomes have not yet been quantified in the supplied resume.
